---
title: Troubleshooting
description: Fixes for the most common problems, and how to send useful logs.
---

Before anything else: close every Roblox window and try again. Turning it off and on again is a classic for a reason.

## Roblox does not launch

- Turn off mods and FastFlags you added recently, then relaunch.
- Use **Force Roblox reinstallation** on **Deployment**.
- Clear the Roblox cache.

## 403 or 404 while downloading

Your deployment channel does not exist right now. Switch back to **Production**.

## Server listing is paused

Roblox is rate limiting requests. Wait a minute.

## Discord does not show your game

Turn on **Share my activity** in Discord and make sure Rich Presence is on in **Integrations**.

## Windows says Voidstrap is a virus

It is a false positive on an unsigned app. Download only from the official site or GitHub releases.

## Sending logs

Use **Export support file** in settings. It packs logs and settings without your logins. Attach it when you [report a bug](/help/report-bug/).
