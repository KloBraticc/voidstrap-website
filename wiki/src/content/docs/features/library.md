## Home

Recent games, quick launch and news. Click a game to join it through the [matchmaker](/features/matchmaker/).

## Library

Every game you played, with playtime and badges earned. Game history keeps the servers you were in so you can rejoin one.

## Accounts

Save several Roblox accounts and switch between them. Logins stay on your PC, encrypted, and are only sent to Roblox.

## Private servers

Lists your private servers and lets you join any of them in one click, or make a desktop shortcut for one from **Shortcuts**.
